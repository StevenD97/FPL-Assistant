Hold an xFPL executive meeting.

Follow `company/MEETING-PROTOCOL.md` exactly. In short:

1. Seat the **minimum** executives the question needs. Name who is not seated and why.
   An executive earns a seat only if they can hold a position the others cannot argue
   on their behalf. Where their input is already in `company/CONTEXT.md` or an existing
   report, cite it as *consulted, not seated*.
2. Each seated executive analyses from their own file in `company/executives/`,
   independently.
3. Allow genuine disagreement. If everyone agrees, say whether the question was trivial
   or the disagreement is being suppressed.
4. Each gives a recommendation with impact, cost, effort, risk and confidence.
5. The CEO evaluates — picks one path, and records each position not taken in that
   executive's own terms, with the trigger that would reopen it.
6. List separately every decision only Steven can make, and what each unblocks.
7. **Stop.** Recommendations only. Implement nothing.

Write the minute to `company/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md` and add a row to
`company/meetings/README.md`.

Reason over documentation that already exists. Do not trigger a fresh repository sweep
unless a seated executive states in the minute what is missing and why the decision
cannot be made without it.
