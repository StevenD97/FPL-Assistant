Now that Gameweek 1 is settled, the shape of it is worth looking at properly rather than reacting to.

## The top of the gameweek

De Cuyper 17. Hinshelwood 16. Mendy 15. Ajayi 14. M.Sangaré 14. Kayode 13. Palmer 13. Stach 13.

One premium in the list. Six defenders or defensive midfielders. Seven of the eight priced under £6.0m at launch, four of them at £4.5m or below.

## Why it happened, mechanically

Two things stacked on the same weekend.

**Five clean sheets from ten fixtures.** That is high, and every clean sheet is four points before anything else happens. On its own it lifts a defender to six or seven.

**The bonus concentrated rather than spread.** In each of the big hauls, the three bonus points went to the defensive players inside the clean sheet rather than being split across a back four and a goalkeeper. Tzolakis took 6 bonus across the weekend from the Hull goal alone. That is the difference between a 6-point clean sheet and a 15-point haul, and it is not a coincidence: defensive contribution scoring rewards the tackles, interceptions and clearances that pile up in exactly the games where a side is defending a lead.

Look at **Szoboszlai's 8 points at Newcastle** for the version of this without a clean sheet at all. A holding midfielder can now return meaningfully from a 2-2 draw. That was not true two seasons ago.

## What it means for a squad, and what it doesn't

It does **not** mean sell your premiums after one blank. Haaland has more hauls in him than any player in the game, and a manager who sold him after Gameweek 1 last season spent the rest of it regretting it.

What it does mean is that the money at the top of a squad now has a higher bar to clear. If a £4.0m defender can plausibly return 8-10 points in a good week rather than 2-4, the gap a £15.5m forward has to make up is bigger than the old arithmetic assumed. The bench and the fifth defender are no longer dead slots you fill with the cheapest legal body.

## The two genuine problems from the weekend

Not the blanks — the absences.

**Gyökeres** played no minutes in Arsenal's opener. **Watkins** played no minutes in Villa's. Both are forwards priced above £7.0m. A blank is noise; zero minutes twice becomes a role question, and role questions do not fix themselves.

Check both on [Players](/players) before the Friday deadline rather than after it.

## The one number I'd actually plan around

Hull have played one game and kept a clean sheet against Manchester United, and their back line costs £4.0-4.5m. Their next fixture is away at Coventry — another promoted side. That is not a reason to buy three Hull defenders. It is a reason to look at whether your own fifth defender and bench are earning anything at all, because this weekend they could have.
