The season is live. Arsenal 3-0 Coventry at the Emirates, and the Fantasy story of the night is not the scoreline — it's who took the points off it.

## Ben White and Ødegaard, 11 points each

The two best returns in the game on opening night were **Ben White (11)** and **Martin Ødegaard (11)**. **Calafiori** and **Saka** followed on 9 apiece. That's a top four with two defenders in it, from a side that scored three.

White's is the one worth sitting with. Eleven points from a defender in a clean sheet means an attacking return plus bonus, and it came from a player who started the season at £5.5m — below Calafiori, and a long way below the price of the forwards most squads spent up on. Ødegaard at £6.5m is in the same bracket. Neither is a name that dominated pre-season squad-building conversation.

## Saka came off on 67 minutes

Saka's 9 points came in 67 minutes before he was withdrawn. Not an injury note — a comfortable scoreline and a manager with a season to manage. But if you captained him, that's the difference between a very good night and a great one, decided by a substitution you had no way to price in.

## Gyökeres: zero minutes

**Gyökeres did not play.** Not benched-and-brought-on; no minutes at all in Arsenal's opening fixture. For a £7.5m forward at a side that just scored three at home, that is the most expensive non-event of the night, and it will be the first thing his owners check on Saturday morning.

One fixture is not a pattern. But "expensive attacker at a big club, zero minutes in the opener" is the exact shape of the early-season trap that costs a manager two transfers by Gameweek 4 — one to bring him in, one to get rid.

## Coventry: nothing, and that's fine

Coventry came away with no returns worth the name. Their opening month was always going to be hard, and 3-0 at the Emirates is not the game to judge a promoted side on. **Thomas** at £4.0m is the one to watch rather than react to.

## What it changes

Very little yet — this is one game of thirty-eight, and reacting to a single fixture is how you burn a free transfer in the first week of the season. But two things are worth writing down:

- Arsenal's clean sheet came with attacking returns from *defenders*, which is the combination that makes a cheap Arsenal defender worth more than the price band suggests.
- A forward who blanked is a form question. A forward who did not get on the pitch is a question about their role, and that is a different kind of problem.
