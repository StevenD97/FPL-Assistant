## The three that matter

**Man City v Coventry (Sat 14:00).** The kindest fixture of the round on paper — a promoted side with one point from two, away at a City side that just scored four. If you have been waiting for the week to captain Haaland, the fixture difficulty says this is it, and our model agrees: it rates this the highest single-gameweek score available to a City asset in the scanned window.

**Hull v Aston Villa (Sat 16:30).** Hull are two from two with two clean sheets and their entire back line still costs £4.0-4.5m. Villa have scored nothing in two gameweeks and no longer have Watkins. A third clean sheet is genuinely live, and the assets are almost free.

**Arsenal v Chelsea (Sun 15:30).** The hardest fixture of the round for both sides — the FDR rates it 4 and 5. Arsenal have two clean sheets from two; Chelsea have just been in a 4-3. If you own Calafiori, Saka, Palmer or João Pedro, this is the one where the projection and the eye test pull in different directions, and it kicks off last on Sunday.

## The captaincy shape of the week

Two things are structurally true regardless of who you pick:

- A **Friday** captain (Ipswich v Liverpool) locks your decision before nine fixtures have been played.
- A **Sunday 15:30** captain (Arsenal v Chelsea) is the last one to resolve — which means every other manager's score is settled while yours isn't.

Neither is right or wrong; they are different risk shapes, and the difference is bigger than most of the gap between the candidates themselves.
