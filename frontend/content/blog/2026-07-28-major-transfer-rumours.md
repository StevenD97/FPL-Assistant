Monday's [transfer watch piece](/blog/2026-07-26-preseason-transfer-watch) covered what's actually been signed, sealed, and priced. This one's about what's still just talk - three stories big enough that, if any of them land before the window closes on 31 August, they'd genuinely change how a squad should be built.

## Arsenal chasing Vinicius Jr

The eyebrow-raiser of the window: **Arsenal**, reigning Premier League champions, are exploring a move for **Vinicius Junior**. According to David Ornstein, the club have agreed "at all levels" to pursue a deal, and are reportedly ready to make him the highest earner in Arsenal's history if it happens. It's genuinely early stage, though - the two clubs aren't yet in talks, and the underlying reason it's even possible is that Vinicius's Real Madrid contract runs out next summer with no extension agreed yet. He's also not the only forward Arsenal are said to be exploring, alongside Julian Alvarez, Yan Diomande, and Bradley Barcola.

**Fantasy read:** worth knowing, not worth planning around. A deal this early stage, tied to a contract situation that isn't even resolved until next summer, isn't a Gameweek 1 input - and Arsenal's front line is already stacked without him. File this one under "watch," not "wait for."

## Man Utd's third midfielder: Carlos Baleba

Manchester United have reopened talks for **Carlos Baleba**, Brighton's Cameroon defensive midfielder, after missing out on him last year. It would be United's third midfield addition of the summer, after **Youri Tielemans** and **Andrey Santos** already arrived - Brighton are understood to want a fee in the region of the £115m Chelsea paid for Moisés Caicedo two years ago, and Baleba himself is said to be keen on the move.

**Fantasy read:** a defensive midfielder isn't an attacking-returns pickup even if this goes through, so it's not a name to chase for your own squad. What it is worth watching is the knock-on effect: a third midfield signing on top of two already in means more competition for minutes around **Bruno Fernandes** in Michael Carrick's engine room - worth checking status before assuming any Man Utd midfielder beyond Fernandes himself is a nailed starter once the season's underway.

## Richarlison could be on his way out of Tottenham

The most directly FPL-relevant of the three: **Richarlison** is reportedly available at a cut-price fee, with Tottenham said to be willing to accept around €20-25m (£17-21m) - well down on the £60m they paid Everton for him in 2022 - rather than risk losing him for nothing when his contract expires next summer. **Juventus** and **Fenerbahçe** are both interested, though he's said to prefer staying in the Premier League or another leading European competition if a genuine alternative exists.

**Fantasy read:** if you already own or were considering Richarlison, this is the one to actually track - a sale before Gameweek 1 would free up minutes for whoever's next in line up front at Spurs, which matters more for squad planning than either of the two moves above. Worth checking [Players](/players) for his status in the run-up to deadline day rather than locking him into a starting XI on the assumption nothing changes.

## The general rule

None of these are confirmed, and rumours this early in a window die far more often than they land. The pattern worth taking from all three: don't build a squad around what a player *might* do next month - build it around what's actually happened, and revisit before each deadline if a genuine deal breaks.
