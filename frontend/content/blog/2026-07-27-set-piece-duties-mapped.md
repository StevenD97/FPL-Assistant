Penalty and free-kick duty is one of the clearest edges Fantasy managers can find before a ball's even been kicked - it's not a projection, it's a confirmed role. Pulling this site's live player data turns up a full picture across the league: every club has a first-choice penalty taker and free-kick taker locked in for 2026/27. Corners are a different story entirely.

## Penalties: all 20 clubs confirmed

Every club now has a nailed-on penalty taker, and most are exactly who you'd expect: **Saka** (Arsenal), **Palmer** (Chelsea), **Haaland** (Man City), **B.Fernandes** (Man Utd), and **Mateta** (Crystal Palace) are all first-choice at their clubs again this season, each with a backup already confirmed too in case of injury or rotation - Watkins is second in line at Villa, Gyökeres backs up Saka at Arsenal, and Mbeumo is Man Utd's second option behind Fernandes.

The promoted three all have their own men on the spot: **Wright** at Coventry, **Crooks** at Hull, and **Hirst** at Ipswich - worth knowing if you're weighing up a cheap differential from any of the three, since penalty involvement is exactly the kind of extra returns that make a budget player worth a second look.

## The one that's actually changed: Liverpool

The most significant shift isn't a new signing taking over penalties at a smaller club - it's at Liverpool. **Alexander Isak** now holds first-choice penalty duty there, with **Szoboszlai** second in line. Mohamed Salah, Liverpool's penalty taker for the better part of a decade, doesn't appear anywhere in the current player pool at all - he left Liverpool this summer with a year still left on his contract, and looks close to a move to Turkiye after interest from Saudi Arabia and MLS earlier in the window. It's a genuinely rare situation: a club's most reliable source of nailed-on penalty points changing hands entirely, not just shuffling between two existing squad players. Worth factoring directly into how you value Isak specifically, not just Liverpool's front line in general.

## Free-kicks: also fully assigned, with a couple of notable absences

Direct free-kick duty is confirmed everywhere too, and it's a more varied list than penalties - **Rice** (Arsenal), **B.Fernandes** (Man Utd), and **Xhaka** (Sunderland) lead their clubs, while a few defenders get a mention as well: **James** at Chelsea, **Porro** at Spurs, **Hall** at Newcastle. Two of the names on the list carry a fitness flag worth knowing before you build around them: Everton's **Garner** (groin injury, expected back 22 August - so likely to miss the opener) and Coventry's **Rudoni** (75% chance of playing, listed as a doubt). Neither changes who's *listed* as first-choice, but it's worth checking [Players](/players) for the latest status before assuming either takes their first free-kick of the season in Gameweek 1.

## Corners: nobody's assigned yet

Here's the genuinely interesting gap: not a single club has a confirmed corner-taker in the data right now. That's not a data error on our end - it's FPL itself not having published corner duty for any team yet, this early in pre-season. It'll fill in as pre-season friendlies and the opening gameweeks give a clearer read on each side's set-piece routines. Worth checking back on this one specifically once Gameweek 1 is behind us - a nailed corner-taker at a corner-heavy side is exactly the kind of quiet edge that's easy to miss if you're not looking for it.

## How to actually use this

None of this should be the only reason to buy a player - but it's real, confirmed information, not a projection, and it stacks on top of whatever the rest of the model already likes about someone. If you're deciding between two similarly-priced options at a position, first-choice set-piece duty is a legitimate tiebreaker. [Players](/players) shows penalty and free-kick order next to every name if you want to check anyone not covered here.
