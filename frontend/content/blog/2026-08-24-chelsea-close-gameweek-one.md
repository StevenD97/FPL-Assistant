Fulham 2-3 Chelsea on Monday night, and Gameweek 1 is done.

## Palmer 13, João Pedro 11

Finally, a fixture where the expensive players did the work. **Palmer 13 points in 82 minutes**, **João Pedro 11 in ninety**, **Rogers 8**. A five-goal game away from home, and Chelsea's front line took what was going.

João Pedro's is the significant one for most squads: he is the single most-owned forward in the game, and after a weekend in which the premium attackers largely failed to return, he delivered. Palmer's 13 is the best midfield return of the gameweek outside Hull's defence.

Fulham's **King** took 7 from the losing side.

## What actually happened

Five clean sheets across the weekend, and the bonus points inside those clean sheets went to full-backs and holding midfielders rather than being split thinly across a back four. That is what turns a 6-point clean sheet into a 15-point haul, and it is the mechanism behind almost every big return of the gameweek.

The premiums, collectively, had a poor weekend: Haaland 2, Isak 2, B.Fernandes 2, Mbeumo 2, Thiago 0, Gyökeres 0 (no minutes), Watkins 0 (no minutes). Palmer and João Pedro were the exceptions.

## Before you react

The temptation after a gameweek like this is to sell the expensive players who blanked and buy the cheap defenders who hauled. Both halves of that trade are usually wrong after one fixture — you would be selling on the worst possible price and buying at the highest, on a sample of one game.

The thing genuinely worth acting on is the pair of zero-minute forwards. A player who blanked is a player who might return next week. A player who did not get on the pitch is a question about their role, and that question does not resolve itself by waiting.
