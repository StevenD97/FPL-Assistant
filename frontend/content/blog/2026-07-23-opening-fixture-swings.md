Fixture difficulty is the one piece of Gameweek 1 planning that's already fully locked in - no projection needed, just the calendar. Working through the Premier League's own Fixture Difficulty Ratings for the opening six gameweeks turns up a clearer picture than "check the colours and squint."

## The kindest runs

Four teams share the best average difficulty for their first six:

- **Everton**: CRY (H), BOU (A), MUN (H), TOT (A), IPS (H), HUL (A)
- **Liverpool**: NEW (A), NFO (H), IPS (A), FUL (H), BOU (A), MCI (H)
- **Man Utd**: HUL (A), IPS (H), EVE (A), MCI (H), FUL (A), TOT (H)
- **Newcastle**: LIV (H), TOT (A), BOU (H), LEE (A), HUL (H), COV (A)

Worth noting straight away: Liverpool and Newcastle play *each other* in Gameweek 1, and Man Utd and Everton meet in Gameweek 3 - two of these four "easy runs" partly cancel each other out, which the average alone doesn't tell you. Man Utd's run is the standout on closer inspection: four of their first six are against sides that finished well outside the top half last time out, with the only real tests (Man City, Spurs) both at home.

## The tough end

**Bournemouth** sits alone at the bottom of the run-in: MCI (A), EVE (H), NEW (A), BRE (H), LIV (H), CHE (A). Three of the league's stronger sides in their first six, two of them away, is a genuinely rough draw to build around - not a reason to avoid Bournemouth's better players outright, but a reason to expect a slow start from their defence in particular.

**Fulham** aren't far behind, and a cluster of **Brentford, Coventry, Hull, and Leeds** round out the harder end together. The promoted sides among them - Coventry and Hull both facing a tough opening run on top of the step up in quality - is worth weighing carefully if you're tempted by a cheap price tag from either club.

## How to actually use this

Fixture difficulty on its own is a blunt instrument - it doesn't know a team just sold its best striker, or that a "hard" fixture is away at a side missing three defenders to injury. It's one input, not the answer, but the calendar itself doesn't lie: Man Utd open against two of their easiest fixtures of the six before the run toughens up around Gameweek 4, which is exactly the kind of detail worth building a transfer plan around now, before the rest of the FPL crowd catches up.
