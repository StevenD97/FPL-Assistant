Fixture difficulty is the one piece of Gameweek 1 planning that's already fully live - no prediction needed, just the calendar. Pulling the numbers straight from this site's own [Fixtures](/fixtures) model for the opening six gameweeks turns up a clearer picture than "check the colours and squint."

## The kindest runs

Four teams are tied at the top on average difficulty for their first six:

- **Liverpool** (avg 2.83): NEW (A), NFO (H), IPS (A), FUL (H), BOU (A), MCI (H)
- **Man Utd** (avg 2.83): HUL (A), IPS (H), EVE (A), MCI (H), FUL (A), TOT (H)
- **Newcastle** (avg 2.83): LIV (H), TOT (A), BOU (H), LEE (A), HUL (H), COV (A)
- **Everton** (avg 2.83): CRY (H), BOU (A), MUN (H), TOT (A), IPS (H), HUL (A)

Worth noting straight away: Liverpool and Newcastle play *each other* in Gameweek 1, and Man Utd and Everton meet in Gameweek 3 - two of these four "easy runs" partly cancel each other out, which the average alone doesn't tell you. Man Utd's run is the standout on closer inspection: four of their first six are against sides currently projected in the bottom half, with the only real tests (Man City, Spurs) both at home.

## The tough end

**Bournemouth** sits alone at the bottom (avg 3.67): MCI (A), EVE (H), NEW (A), BRE (H), LIV (H), CHE (A). Three of the league's stronger sides in their first six, two of them away, is a genuinely rough draw to build around - it's not a reason to avoid Bournemouth's better players outright, but it's a reason to expect a slow start from their defence in particular.

**Fulham** (avg 3.33), and a cluster of **Coventry, Brentford, Leeds, and Hull** (all avg 3.17), round out the harder end. The promoted sides among them (Coventry, Hull) facing a tough run on top of the step up in quality is worth weighing carefully - see our [promoted-teams piece](/blog/2026-07-24-promoted-three-scouted) for more on that.

## How to actually use this

Fixture difficulty on its own is a blunt instrument - it doesn't know a team just sold its best striker, or that a "hard" fixture is away at a side missing three defenders to injury. It's one input, not the answer. The [Outlook](/outlook) page combines this same fixture data with the site's points model to rank actual transfer targets over a chosen window, which is the more useful next step once you've got a shortlist of teams worth targeting from the run-down above.
