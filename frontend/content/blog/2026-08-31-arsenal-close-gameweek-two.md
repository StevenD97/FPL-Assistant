Aston Villa 0-1 Arsenal on Monday night. Saka scored it, Calafiori made it, and Gameweek 2 is done.

## Calafiori 11, Saka 11

**Calafiori: assist, clean sheet, 2 bonus — 11 points.** **Saka: goal, clean sheet, 3 bonus — 11 points.** **Gabriel 8**, **White 7**.

Arsenal have now kept clean sheets in both gameweeks, and in both, their defenders took attacking returns on top. Calafiori has 20 points from two games at £5.5m. White has 18 at the same price. Saka has 20.

Gabriel's 8 came with **10 defensive contributions** in the match — the tackles, interceptions and clearances that now score directly. That is a centre-back producing a returning score without touching the ball in the opposition box.

## Villa: nothing, again

No returns of note, no goals, and a second gameweek in which the most expensive thing about Aston Villa for Fantasy purposes was the player who is no longer there. Anyone still carrying **Watkins** has now held a non-playing asset through two full gameweeks and a price fall.

## Gameweek 2, closed

Ten fixtures. The headline numbers:

- **Cherki 14** — the highest-scoring midfielder under £8.0m.
- **Groß 13**, **Gibbs-White 13**, **Haaland 13** — the chasing pack.
- **Hall 11**, **Dedić 11**, **Calafiori 11**, **Saka 11** — four defenders on eleven.

## Two gameweeks in, what's actually true

**Cheap defence is scoring.** Hull's back line at £4.0-4.5m, Arsenal's at £5.5m, Newcastle's full-backs, Everton's centre-back. Six of the top twelve scorers in the game are defenders or goalkeepers.

**The premiums are fine, but not obviously better.** Haaland: 2 then 13. B.Fernandes: 2 then 23. Both have exactly one good week and one bad one, which is what premiums do — the question is only whether the price is worth the variance when the cheap slots are returning this well.

**Two forwards have played no minutes at all.** Gyökeres and Watkins. If either is still in your squad, that is your transfer this week and it has been for a fortnight.
