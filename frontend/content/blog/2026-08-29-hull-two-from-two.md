Saturday's four fixtures, in order.

## Liverpool 2-2 Nottingham Forest (11:30)

**Gibbs-White 13** was the best return of the day and the clearest reminder that a £7.9m midfielder at a mid-table club can beat almost anything at twice the price on the right afternoon. **Ndoye 9** alongside him.

Liverpool: **Isak 8** — an improvement on his opening two — and **Munoz 6**. Two draws from two at home for a side a lot of squads built a defence around, and no clean sheet in either.

## Bournemouth 1-1 Everton (14:00)

**Scott 12** for Bournemouth and **Tarkowski 12** for Everton, from a 1-1. Tarkowski's is the more instructive: a centre-back returning double figures without a clean sheet, on defensive contribution and a goal. Everton have now produced a big defensive return in both gameweeks.

## Coventry 0-1 Hull (14:00)

**Two from two for Hull, and two clean sheets from two.** Tzolakis 10, Egan 9, Ajayi 6 — a goalkeeper at £4.5m and defenders at £4.0m, again.

After beating Manchester United at home, they have gone to a fellow promoted side and won 1-0. Six points, no goals conceded. Whatever anyone expected from Hull in August, this was not it, and the entire back line is still priced as though it were.

Coventry's **Thomas 8** was the best of the home side, which is worth noting if you are looking for a £4.0m defender who is not at Hull.

## Tottenham 0-2 Newcastle (16:30)

**Hall 11 and Dedić 11** — both full-backs, both in the same clean sheet, both double figures. That is now the third fixture in two gameweeks where the bonus inside a clean sheet has gone to the wide defenders rather than being spread across the back four.

**Elanga 8** and **Wissa 8** made it four Newcastle returns from one game. Tottenham have now conceded five and scored none across two gameweeks.

## The thread running through all of it

Nine of the twelve best returns today were defenders, goalkeepers or holding midfielders. Two gameweeks in, that is no longer a quirk of an opening weekend — it is the shape of this season's scoring so far.

The corollary is worth stating plainly: if your bench and fifth defender are £4.0m players chosen because they were the cheapest bodies that fit, they are currently the slots where the market is mispricing points.

Four more fixtures tomorrow, including Manchester United v Ipswich.
