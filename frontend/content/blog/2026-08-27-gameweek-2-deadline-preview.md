Gameweek 2 deadline: **Friday 28 August, 17:30**. Crystal Palace v Manchester City opens it at 19:00.

## The trap this week

Gameweek 1 produced eight double-figure returns, seven of them from players under £6.0m. Every one of those players is now more expensive and more owned than they were on Friday, and none of them has a fixture that guarantees a repeat.

Buying De Cuyper after 17 points is buying at the top of the market on a sample of one. It might still work. But it is a transfer made because of what already happened, and those are the ones that cost you a free transfer and then a hit to undo.

## The transfer that is actually worth making

If you own **Watkins** or **Gyökeres**, that is your transfer.

Both played zero minutes in Gameweek 1. Not benched-and-introduced — no minutes at all. Both are priced above £7.0m. Whatever the reason, a squad slot that produced nothing and is not certain to start is the worst thing to hold through a second gameweek, because the price will fall while you wait to find out.

Check both on [Players](/players) for their current status before you decide — the flags and news there come straight from FPL and update through the day.

Everything else can wait. One gameweek is not enough evidence to move a player you spent real thought on in pre-season.

## Captaincy

The honest position is that our own model called this badly last week — 2 points from its top pick, 148th in the game. That doesn't make the process wrong, but it does mean this week's suggestion deserves your own sanity check rather than blind trust.

The two structural things worth weighing:

- **Manchester City open the gameweek away at Crystal Palace on Friday night.** A Friday captain is a captain you cannot change your mind about once the rest of the weekend starts. That is a real cost, separate from whether the pick is good.
- **Manchester United host Ipswich on Sunday afternoon.** A promoted side away at Old Trafford is the kind of fixture that produces a big attacking return, and Fernandes takes the penalties.

[My Squad](/squad) will give you the projection for your own eleven, with the reasoning next to each call.

## Chips: not yet

Nobody should be playing a chip in Gameweek 2. Bench Boost wants a squad where all fifteen have good fixtures, Triple Captain wants a specific week you have identified in advance, and Free Hit wants a blank gameweek. None of those conditions exist yet, and the fixture calendar this early gives you no information you won't have more of in six weeks.

If a tool is telling you to play a chip in the second week of the season, be suspicious of the tool.
