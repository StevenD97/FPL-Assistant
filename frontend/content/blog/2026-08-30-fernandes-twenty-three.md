Four fixtures on Sunday and one enormous score.

## Manchester United 5-2 Ipswich (15:30)

**B.Fernandes: 23 points.**

The highest single return of the season so far, by seven points. Goals, assists, bonus, ninety minutes. A week after taking 2 points from a defeat at Hull, he produced the sort of haul that decides a gameweek rank on its own.

**Mbeumo 11** and **Cunha 9** made it three double-figure-or-close returns from one fixture. Ipswich's **Davis 6** was the visitors' best.

If you captained him you have had a very good Sunday. If you own him and captained elsewhere, this is the specific pain of a 48%-owned premium: you took the ownership risk and none of the reward.

## Chelsea 4-3 Brighton (13:00)

Seven goals, and the top return came from the losing side: **Groß 13**. **João Pedro 9**, **Yalcouyé 8**, **Palmer 7**.

Nobody's defence came out of this one with anything. If you owned Chelsea or Brighton defenders — and after De Cuyper's 17 last week a lot of squads had just bought one — this is the game that shows why buying a defender for the attacking upside is a different bet from buying one for clean sheets.

## Sunderland 1-0 Fulham (13:00)

**Mukiele 9, Ballard 8, Isidor 8.** Another 1-0, another clean sheet, another set of defensive returns from a side almost nobody owns. Fulham's **Castagne 6** from the losing side.

## Leeds 1-1 Brentford (13:00)

**Schade 10** the pick of it, **Calvert-Lewin 8** for Leeds. A quieter fixture than the rest.
