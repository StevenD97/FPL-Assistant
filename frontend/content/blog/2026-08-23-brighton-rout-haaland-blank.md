Three fixtures on Sunday, and the gap between what the cheap defenders did and what the expensive forwards did got wider.

## Brighton 4-0 Aston Villa (13:00)

**De Cuyper 17. Hinshelwood 16.**

Seventeen points from a defender who started the season at £4.5m is the highest single return of the gameweek so far, and Hinshelwood's 16 is not far behind. Four goals, a clean sheet, and the bonus points landing on the full-backs rather than the front line.

Villa's afternoon needs a separate note. **Watkins did not play** — zero minutes — and it is worth checking his status before you assume that's rotation. Anyone still holding him is holding a slot, not a player.

## Man City 2-1 Bournemouth (13:00)

City won. Here is the Fantasy scoreboard from it:

Ninety minutes, a home win, and two points. The best return in a Manchester City fixture belonged to a Bournemouth player, and the best City returns were two defenders and a midfielder who cost a combined fraction of the striker.

**Cherki's 8 came in 27 minutes.** That is the number to sit with rather than Haaland's 2. A player producing a returning performance off the bench in under half an hour is a player whose minutes are about to go up.

## Newcastle 2-2 Liverpool (15:30)

The best game of the weekend and a genuinely useful one for Fantasy. **Gakpo 12** was the standout; **Elanga 9** for Newcastle; **Szoboszlai 8** — a defensive midfielder returning without a clean sheet, which is the defensive-contribution scoring doing its job.

**Isak: 2 points in ninety minutes.** The two most expensive forwards in the game both played a full match this weekend and returned two points each.

## The honest read on the premiums

One gameweek. Haaland has scored two goals in a fixture more times than most players have scored at all, and one blank against a mid-table side proves nothing about the rest of the season. The same is true of Isak.

But the arithmetic of the weekend is not nothing either: a manager who spent £15.5m on Haaland and £4.5m on De Cuyper has, so far, been paid seventeen points by the cheap one and two by the expensive one. The cheap defensive slots are not behaving like cheap defensive slots this season, and that changes what the money at the top of a squad has to earn to justify itself.

One fixture left in Gameweek 1: Fulham v Chelsea tomorrow night.
