Saturday gave us five fixtures and one genuine shock, and the shock was also the best Fantasy return of the day by a distance.

## Hull 2-0 Manchester United (11:30)

**Mendy 15. Ajayi 14. Tzolakis 10. Egan 8.**

A promoted side beat Manchester United at home, kept a clean sheet doing it, and handed four returns of eight or more to owners who had spent almost nothing. Mendy and Egan are £4.0m. Ajayi is £4.1m. Tzolakis, the goalkeeper, is £4.5m. You could have had all four for £16.6m and taken 47 points out of a single fixture.

That is not a normal opening weekend. Fifteen points from a £4.0m defender means a goal or assist on top of the clean sheet and bonus, and it is exactly the kind of return that the cheap-enabler slots in a squad are *supposed* to occasionally produce and almost never do.

For Manchester United owners: two points from **B.Fernandes** and two from **Mbeumo**, both playing the full ninety. No returns, no clean sheet, nothing to show for £20m of squad value.

## Brentford 3-0 Tottenham (16:30)

The other rout. **M.Sangaré 14**, **Kayode 13**, **Lewis-Potter 10**, **Janelt 9** — again, four returns from one fixture, again from a side nobody built around. Sangaré at £5.5m and Kayode at £4.5m were the two most valuable midfield and defensive returns of the afternoon.

Tottenham: nothing. Three goals conceded, no clean sheet, no attacking return worth listing.

## The 14:00 kick-offs

- **Everton 2-0 Crystal Palace.** Dewsbury-Hall 11, Ndiaye 9, Armstrong 8, Barry 8. Another clean sheet, another set of mid-price returns.
- **Ipswich 2-1 Sunderland.** Emersonn 9 for the home side; **Xhaka 9** was Sunderland's, which is a reminder that a defensive midfielder on free-kicks at a mid-table club is a real Fantasy asset and not just a squad filler.
- **Nottingham Forest 0-1 Leeds.** **Stach 13**, Bijol 10, Justin 10, Trafford 9. A one-goal away win produced four double-figure-adjacent returns because the clean sheet did the work.

## The pattern nobody planned for

Look at the day's best returns: Mendy, Ajayi, Sangaré, Kayode, Stach, Bijol, Justin, Dewsbury-Hall. Not one of them is a premium. Six of the eight are defenders or defensive midfielders, and every single one came attached to a clean sheet.

Five of Saturday's ten sides kept a clean sheet. That is a high rate for an opening weekend, and it is the single most useful thing to take from the day — not "buy Hull defenders", but that the points on offer in the cheap defensive slots this season are not the four-point floor everyone budgets for.
